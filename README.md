A code-drawn 1024×1024 poster summing up the world's news of Tuesday
24 November 2026 in one image — six verified stories, from the Huawei
verdict in Brooklyn to the tankers hit in the Strait of Hormuz.

- `draw_poster.py` draws `entry.png` with Pillow (deterministic, no network).
- `main.py` is the entry program: it copies `entry.png` to the output.
- `NEWS.md` lists the six stories and their sources.

Built for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/r/4q7hssxn) bounty
`news-image-20261124`.
