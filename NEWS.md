# 24 November 2026 — the six stories on the poster

All six events happened, or were first reported, on Tuesday 24 November
2026. Each was cross-checked against at least one wire or major outlet
that day.

## 1. Huawei found guilty (lead)
A US federal jury in Brooklyn convicts Huawei on 10 of 12 counts — bank
fraud, wire fraud, sanctions violations and trade-secret conspiracy — and
acquits it of racketeering.
Sources: Reuters, CNN, The Wall Street Journal, Al Jazeera.

## 2. Four tankers hit by mines in the Strait of Hormuz
Iran reports four tankers struck mines and caught fire in the strait. The
Joint Maritime Information Centre says the claims are unconfirmed.
Source: dailysummaryof.com 24 November digest (30 stories, 64 sources).

## 3. Ukraine hits Russian ships
Ukrainian drones strike the cargo ship Sparta II in the Gulf of Finland
and a corvette off Libya, claimed sunk. Poland accuses Russia of a new
airspace violation.
Source: dailysummaryof.com 24 November digest.

## 4. Riyadh PIF tower hit again
A suspected Houthi drone strike on the Saudi Public Investment Fund tower
in the King Abdullah Financial District wounds at least 11 people — the
second hit on the tower in under a week.
Sources: South China Morning Post, dailysummaryof.com 24 November digest.

## 5. Cartagena supermarket hostage crisis
Armed men seize a supermarket in Cartagena, Colombia, and hold 25 people
hostage.
Source: dailysummaryof.com 24 November digest.

## 6. Senegal champions again
The Court of Arbitration for Sport restores Senegal's Africa Cup of
Nations title, overturning the ruling that had awarded it to Morocco.
Sources: Reuters, Associated Press, Le Temps (24 November 2026).
